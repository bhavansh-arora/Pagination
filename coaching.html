Transition coaching

When who you are and what you do stop matching.

For people who no longer accept the cognitive dissonance within them. Awaken your creativity, align your values with your work, and find the clarity and courage to make the move.

For you if

  • Your career looks good on paper, but feels wrong
  • You’re craving meaning, not just a new title
