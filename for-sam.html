(01)Today vs. the preview

Same heart. More bookings.

We kept Square as your payment system, so there's nothing new for you to learn or set up at the till.

Today

A good-looking shop page

  • Classes listed as shop products, with no calendar or seat counts
  • Full classes simply disappear, and the interest is lost
  • Private lessons and group bookings handled by email and chat
  • Generic Square gift card with no personal touch

The preview

A booking engine with a beautiful front

  • Live calendar and "3 of 8 seats left" on every date, which creates urgency
  • Automatic waitlist, so cancellations refill themselves

  • Designer gift vouchers with personal messages and scheduled delivery
  • Event & local-business schema, so classes show in Google with dates and prices
