A few things worth clicking:

- **Live class calendar.** Every date shows seats left ("2 of 8 left"), so people book instead of messaging to ask if there's space.
- **Book and pay in under a minute.** Apple Pay, Google Pay or card, through your existing Square account, so there's nothing new to set up.
- **Automatic waitlist.** When a class sells out, people join a list, and a cancelled seat goes straight to the next person.
- **Calendar invites and reminders.** Every booking gets an "add to calendar" file and a reminder with directions, which means fewer no-shows.
- **Private-lesson planner.** Guests choose a format, bakes and group size and see an estimate before they enquire, so the enquiries that reach you are ready to book.
- **Gift vouchers.** Any amount or a specific class, with a personal message, delivered on the day they choose. They sell well all year and especially at Christmas.
- **Bakery pre-orders.** Customers order ahead and choose a collection day, so you bake to order with nothing wasted.
- **Found on Google.** Each class date is set up to appear in search with its price and availability.

Your current site already looks lovely, so this isn't about starting over. It's about letting the website handle the bookings and admin while you're in the kitchen.
