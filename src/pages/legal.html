Our fees

Your initial appointment is free. There may be a fee for arranging your mortgage, and the precise amount will depend on your circumstances. We typically charge £295, but this will not exceed £1,000. We will confirm the fee at your initial appointment, before you commit to anything.

There may also be a fee for equity release advice, which will be disclosed at your initial appointment.

Risk warnings

  • Your home may be repossessed if you do not keep up repayments on your mortgage.
  • Early repayment charges may apply if you repay your mortgage early or switch during a deal period.
  • Equity release may involve a home reversion plan or lifetime mortgage secured against your property. It will reduce the value of your estate and may affect your entitlement to means-tested benefits.
  • Not all buy-to-let and commercial mortgages are regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority.
  • Calculators on this website provide illustrations only and are not an offer of credit or financial advice.

Fraud alert

We will never ask for your bank details in relation to paying your deposit. Your solicitor may request payments directly, but always confirm independently, preferably by phone, before you transfer any funds.

Privacy notice

We collect and use your personal information to provide mortgage, protection and equity release advice, to communicate with you and to meet our legal and regulatory obligations. We only share your information with lenders, insurers, our principal firm and other parties where it's needed to provide our service, or where the law requires it.

You have the right to access, correct or ask us to delete your personal data, and to object to how we use it. To make a request, email mark@mdjmortgages.com. Our full privacy policy is available on request.
