Good questions

Things couples ask

Can't see yours? Email sam@alphavisuals.co.nz and I'll get back to you personally.

How much is the deposit?

A non-refundable $1,100 NZD deposit locks in your date, and nobody else can book it after that. The balance is settled closer to the wedding.

How long until our film is delivered?

Usually 8–12 weeks. Every film is edited by hand, and it takes time to do your day justice. If you can't wait, add the Next-day Teaser and you'll have a short film within 24 hours.

How many hours do you film on the day?

There are no set coverage hours. I'm there for whatever your story needs, from getting ready through to the first dance, and I plan the timing around your photographer's schedule.

How do you work with our photographer?

As one team. The photographer usually leads, and I add the video-specific shots alongside them. Most of what I film is candid, so you won't be asked to do everything twice.

Can we pick the music?

Suggestions are always welcome! I do choose the final soundtrack, because the music shapes how I shoot and edit, and it's a big part of what makes the films feel the way they do.

What audio is overlaid in the film?

Usually your vows. Speeches like the best man's are generally left out of the highlight film, but if there's a speech you can't imagine the film without, let's talk. Highlight Film+ also records the full ceremony and speeches.

Do you travel?

Yes, anywhere. I'm based in Christchurch and Adelaide. Travel outside greater Christchurch has costs, which I'll quote upfront when you enquire.

Is the video 4K?

Yes. Every film is shot and delivered in 4K.
