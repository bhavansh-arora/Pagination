(03)Today

Everything I've learned, shared with you.

Today, through Love to Cook in Crieff, I bring together everything I've learned: creating delicious bread and pastries, sharing knowledge teaching cuisine and pâtisserie, and helping others discover confidence and joy in the kitchen.

Classes are deliberately small (never more than eight) because the best way to learn a craft is with someone beside you, adjusting your hands and explaining why, not just how.
