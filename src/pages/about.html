The team

The people you'll actually speak to

Mark
Managing Director

Mark Jones

Mark began his career as an equity release adviser, inspired by memories of his gran's home in Farnham. He founded MDJ Mortgages to give local families the kind of honest, personal advice he'd want for his own.

Outside work you'll find him training in karate and Muay Thai, in the gym, or listening to podcasts. His motto? “It is what it is.”

Jodie
Business Development Manager

Jodie Layzell

Jodie joined MDJ Mortgages in November 2022 after more than 20 years in retail, and brings that customer-first instinct to every client. “My favourite part of the job is when a client finally completes on their mortgage and moves into their new home!”

She loves live gigs, reading, dogs and Glastonbury. Her motto? “No worries.”
