How lenders see your income

Different businesses, different approaches

The lender matters as much as the rate. Choosing the one that assesses your income the right way can make a big difference to how much you can borrow.

Talk to us before your accountant files

How you take income affects how much you can borrow. If a purchase is on the horizon, a quick chat before your year-end can make a real difference.

Can I get a mortgage with one year's accounts?

Yes, some lenders will consider one full year of trading, particularly if you've stayed in the same line of work.

My profits dipped last year. Is that a problem?

Lenders usually average two years or take the lower figure. Some will use the latest year if there's a good reason. We'll match you to the right approach.

Do I need a bigger deposit?

Not necessarily. Self-employed borrowers can access the same loan-to-value products as employed borrowers if their income is assessed fairly.
