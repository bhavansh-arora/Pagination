Remortgage FAQs

Before you switch

When should I start looking?

Around six months before your current deal ends. Many lenders let you secure a new rate that far ahead, and you can usually switch to a better one if rates fall before completion.

Will I pay early repayment charges?

Only if you leave before your current deal ends. We'll check your exit fees and tell you whether switching early is worth it.

Is a product transfer easier?

Often, as there's usually no new affordability check or valuation. But it limits you to one lender's rates, so it's worth comparing.

Will remortgaging affect my credit score?

A full application involves a credit check, which leaves a footprint. We use eligibility checks first where possible to avoid unnecessary searches.
