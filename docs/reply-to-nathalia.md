**What I noticed on co-allies.com today**

1. **Fertility is hard to find.** It's one of four services on the homepage, with no page of its own. A woman searching "fertility coach Montréal" or "IVF emotional support" is unlikely to find you.
2. **Service links lead nowhere.** "Fertilité", "Coaching de Transition" and the other menu items, plus the "Découvrir" button, all go back to the homepage. People who click with intent end up where they started.
3. **The free session is a leap into the unknown.** Every service ends with the same Calendly button, but nothing says what happens in those 30 minutes. For someone anxious or grieving, that uncertainty is often why they don't book.
4. **Trust gaps.** There's no FAQ, and the Testimonials page is still a placeholder.
5. **Small details that cost credibility.** Two different email addresses (info@ and contact@), a leftover "info@monsite.fr" link, and footer icons that point to Wix's own LinkedIn and Instagram instead of yours.

**What the preview does about it**

- **A dedicated Fertility page** written for women trying to conceive, going through IVF, grieving a loss or considering adoption. A "Where are you right now?" selector shows what support looks like at each stage.
- **A one-minute guided breathing exercise** right on the page, so women feel what working with you is like before they book.
- **"Coaching, therapy, medical care: where this fits"**, plus crisis numbers (811, 9-8-8). It builds trust and keeps the page responsible.
- **"Find your path" on the homepage.** Visitors pick the sentence that sounds most like them and are pointed to the right service. Each of your four offers now has a real page.
- **Clear next steps.** A three-step "what happens in your free 30 minutes", a booking button on every page (sticky on phones), and your calendar opening directly on the contact page.
- **Your experience, up front.** Yves Rocher North America (450 people), Propulia Capital, your CTI training and the Inner Development Goals. That's rare depth for a coach, and it now shows.
- **No baby photos, on purpose.** They can hurt women in the middle of infertility or loss, so the imagery is about light, nature and growth instead.
- **Built to be found.** Fertility keywords in titles and descriptions, structured data for Google, fast loading, and fully responsive on phone and tablet.

To be transparent, a few details are educated guesses I'd want you to confirm: whether sessions are online, in person or both; languages; your preferred email; and whether you'd like to show pricing. The preview is in English, and a French or bilingual version is a straightforward next step. When you have client words you're comfortable sharing, there's a section ready for them.
