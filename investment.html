Our philosophy

Real, raw &
deeply personal.

We know how quickly the day moves. With high-end gear, a keen eye for detail and an instinct for anticipating moments before they happen, we work quietly and seamlessly, so you can be fully present while we capture the magic.

The experience

Stress-free,
start to finish.

From the quiet anticipation of getting ready to the emotional vows, the lively reception and the intimate in-between moments, we document your day with a blend of artistic vision and journalistic storytelling. We stay discreet but intentional, guiding portraits when needed and otherwise letting the day unfold naturally.

Afterwards, we carefully curate and edit everything. Photos arrive in a beautiful online gallery, ready to view, download and share. Your film arrives as a cinematic story of the day, plus any extra edits such as the full ceremony.

Good to know

Frequently asked
questions.

Do you travel?

Yes! There is no travel charge for weddings in Chicago, Central Illinois, Milwaukee or Madison, Wisconsin. For other areas we charge a small fee to cover our expenses — and we love an adventure.

What gear do you use?

Because of the hybrid photo + video nature of our work, we've chosen the Sony system for its accurate colour science and cinema-quality video. We also use a DJI Ronin 4D and a DJI Mavic 3 drone.

How far in advance should we book you?

Dates are extremely limited, so we recommend reaching out as soon as possible. We already have weddings booked more than 18 months out.

Do you have insurance?

Yes — full coverage on all of our gear, as well as professional liability insurance.

How many photos will we get?

Short answer: it depends.

Slightly longer answer: the more variety in your wedding day, the more photos you'll have. If all of your events are in one space you might have fewer images than at a venue with lots of different backdrops and scenery.

On average we deliver 100 images per hour. If you do a first look, you can expect about 40% more images than a wedding without one.

How long until we get our images and films back?

Generally your images are delivered within 8–12 weeks. During busy season (September – November) turnaround may be a little longer.

We never outsource our editing — every single image is hand-edited by Dan.

Do you offer engagement photos?

Absolutely. All wedding packages include a complimentary engagement session.
