Key takeaways
  • Local rankings depend on relevance, distance and prominence
  • Your Google Business Profile and reviews drive the map results
  • Your website needs clear service and location pages with consistent contact details
  • Steady, small actions every month beat one big push

When someone nearby searches for what you do, Google shows a map with three businesses, then a list of websites. Local SEO is the work of getting into both. For most small businesses it's the highest-return marketing there is: the people searching are close, ready and looking for exactly what you sell.

How Google decides local rankings

Google has said local results depend mainly on three things:

  • Relevance: how well your business matches the search
  • Distance: how close you are to the searcher or the place named
  • Prominence: how well known and trusted you are, online and off

You can't move your premises, but you can improve relevance and prominence a great deal.

1. Google Business Profile

This is the foundation. A complete, active profile is the biggest single factor for map visibility.

  • Claim and verify it
  • Choose the most specific primary category
  • Fill in every field: hours, services, description, attributes
  • Add photos every week or two
  • Post updates monthly
  • Answer questions

Step-by-step in our Google Business Profile optimisation checklist.

2. Reviews

Reviews influence rankings and whether people choose you once they see you.

  • Ask every happy customer, at the moment they're happiest
  • Send a direct link by text or email
  • Reply to every review within a few days
  • Aim for a steady flow; recency matters

3. Your website

Google reads your site to understand what you do and where.

Essentials

  • Name, address and phone (NAP) in the footer of every page, identical to your profile
  • Location in title tags and headings: "Emergency Plumber in Leeds"
  • A page for each service
  • A page for each key area you serve (how to build them)
  • An embedded map on the contact page
  • Local business schema (schema guide)
  • Fast, mobile-friendly pages (Core Web Vitals)

Content

Write for local customers: local projects, area guides, answers to questions people in your town ask.

4. Citations

Citations are listings of your business details on other sites: Yelp, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Facebook, industry directories, local chambers. They matter less than they once did, but consistency still counts.

  • Make sure your NAP is identical everywhere
  • Claim the major platforms
  • Fix old addresses or phone numbers

5. Local links

Links from other local websites are a strong prominence signal.

  • Sponsor a local team or event
  • Join the chamber of commerce
  • Partner with complementary businesses
  • Get featured in local news
  • Supply a testimonial to a local supplier

6. Behaviour signals

Google notices when people click your listing, ask for directions and call. Good photos, a strong rating and accurate hours all increase those actions.

7. Apple, Bing and AI

Local search isn't only Google.

  • Apple Business Connect powers Apple Maps and Siri
  • Bing Places powers Bing and Copilot, and feeds some AI assistants
  • AI search (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) draws on your website, listings and reviews

Clear facts and consistent details across all of them help. See how to get mentioned in AI search.

Common mistakes

  • Keyword stuffing your business name
  • Different phone numbers on different sites
  • One generic "services" page
  • Ignoring negative reviews
  • Dozens of thin, duplicate location pages
  • A slow, outdated website

How long does it take?

Profile improvements can show within weeks. Building a lead over established competitors usually takes three to six months of consistent effort. See how long does SEO take?

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a physical address to rank locally?

No. Service-area businesses can hide their address and list the areas they cover.

How many reviews do I need?

Enough to beat the competitors shown beside you, with new ones arriving regularly.

Is local SEO different from regular SEO?

It's a branch of it. The same fundamentals apply, with extra weight on your business profile, reviews and location signals.
