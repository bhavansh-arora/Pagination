Key takeaways
  • Career clients want tangible outcomes, so lead with results
  • Niche by industry, career stage or transition type
  • LinkedIn drives most traffic, so your site must match your profile
  • Show a clear process from first call to new role

Career coaching clients are usually motivated and measurable. They want a new role, a promotion, a pivot or the confidence to ask for more. That makes your website easier to write than most coaching sites, as long as you're specific.

Lead with outcomes

Career coaching has something most coaching doesn't: clear results. Use them.

  • "Clients typically land a new role within four months"
  • "From senior engineer to engineering manager in one promotion cycle"
  • Logos of companies clients have moved to (with permission)

Only publish numbers you can back up.

What to put on the homepage

  1. Headline: the feeling and the outcome. "Feeling stuck? Find your flow."
  2. Who you help: in one line.
  3. How it works: three steps.
  4. Results: testimonials with role changes.
  5. Your credentials: ICF level, recruiting or HR background, industries you know.
  6. Call to action: a free 20 to 30 minute call.

Services to consider listing

  • One-to-one career coaching programmes
  • CV and LinkedIn review
  • Interview preparation
  • Salary negotiation coaching
  • Career change discovery
  • Corporate outplacement

Give each its own section or page. Smaller, fixed-price services (like a CV review) are good entry points to longer programmes.

Match your LinkedIn

Most career coaching clients find you on LinkedIn and click through. Your headline, photo and message should be consistent across both. Link your site's booking page in your LinkedIn featured section.

Content that attracts career clients

Write the articles your clients are searching for:

  • "How to change careers at 40"
  • "Signs you've outgrown your job"
  • "How to answer 'tell me about yourself'"
  • "How to negotiate salary without losing the offer"

Each ranks on Google, shares well on LinkedIn and ends with an invitation to book. See how to write a blog post that ranks.

Lead magnets that work

  • CV or LinkedIn headline templates
  • A "stay or go" decision worksheet
  • Interview question bank
  • Salary negotiation scripts
  • A career values quiz

The booking path

Embed a calendar, ask two questions (current role and what they want to change), and send automatic reminders. The full approach is in the discovery call funnel.

B2B: outplacement and corporate programmes

If you work with employers, add a separate page for organisations with case studies, the outcomes you measure and an enquiry form for HR. Corporate buyers need different proof from individual clients.

Frequently asked questions

Should career coaches guarantee results?

No. You can share typical outcomes and client stories, but a job offer depends on factors outside your control.

Do I need separate pages for each service?

Yes, if you want each to rank on Google and to give prospects enough detail to choose.

How do career coaches get clients online?

A mix of LinkedIn content, search-optimised articles, referrals and a website that converts visitors into discovery calls.
