Key takeaways
  • Solar searches are local, so build a strong page for each area you serve
  • Service pages for residential, commercial, battery and EV each target different searches
  • Incentive guides and FAQs capture homeowners early in their research
  • Reviews and a complete Google Business Profile drive map visibility

When a homeowner searches "solar installers near me", three companies appear in the map and a handful below it. Those spots generate a steady stream of exclusive, high-intent leads for free. Getting there is a matter of doing a few things consistently.

What solar customers search

TypeExamples
Installer + location"solar installers Dublin", "solar company Barrie"
Product + location"solar panels Auckland", "home battery San Bernardino"
Cost and value"solar panel cost 2026", "is solar worth it in Ontario"
Incentives"SEAI solar grant", "solar rebates [state]"
Comparison"best solar panels", "Tesla Powerwall alternatives"

Your site needs pages that match each type.

1. Service pages

Build a dedicated page for each service:

  • Residential solar
  • Commercial solar
  • Agricultural solar
  • Battery storage
  • EV charger installation
  • Maintenance and repairs

Each should cover who it's for, how it works, typical costs and savings, incentives, FAQs and a quote form.

2. Service area pages

Create a page for each town, city or county you serve, with unique, useful content: local projects, grid or council details, typical savings, reviews from that area. Avoid copy-and-paste. We explain how in service area pages for solar installers.

3. On-page basics

  • Title tags: "Solar Panel Installers in Cork | Eco Horizon"
  • One H1 with the service and location
  • Clear headings and short paragraphs
  • Internal links between services and areas

See title tags and meta descriptions.

4. Google Business Profile

  • Primary category: "Solar energy company" or "Solar energy equipment supplier"
  • Service areas listed
  • Services with descriptions
  • Weekly project photos
  • Every review answered

Full checklist here.

5. Reviews

Ask on commissioning day, when the customer has just seen the meter run backwards. Send a direct link by text.

Incentive guides

7. Schema markup

Add LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ and Review schema so Google and AI assistants understand exactly what you do and where. See our schema markup guide.

8. Speed and mobile

Many solar sites are weighed down by chat widgets, tracking scripts and uncompressed drone footage. Trim them. Aim for green Core Web Vitals on mobile. See Core Web Vitals explained.

9. Links

  • Manufacturer "find an installer" directories
  • Accreditation bodies
  • Local chambers of commerce and business groups
  • Local news stories about community or commercial projects
  • Supplier and partner websites

10. Be visible in AI answers

Homeowners now ask ChatGPT and Google's AI Overviews "who are the best solar installers in [area]?" Clear facts on your site (areas served, certifications, years trading, review counts), consistent listings and FAQ content help you get mentioned. See how to get mentioned in AI search.

Frequently asked questions

How long until SEO brings leads?

Map visibility and long-tail terms can improve in a few weeks; competitive city terms usually take four to nine months.

Should I target "solar panels" nationally?

No. Local terms convert better and are far easier to win.

Do I need a blog?

You need useful content. Guides, FAQs and case studies matter more than a traditional blog.
