25 Solar FAQs Your Website Should Answer

The 25 questions homeowners ask before buying solar, grouped by topic, with guidance on answering them to build trust, rank on Google and appear in AI answers.

Key takeaways
  • Every FAQ is a search query and an objection you can remove
  • Answer in two to four plain sentences, then link to detail
  • Group questions by topic so visitors find theirs quickly
  • FAQ schema helps answers appear in search results and AI summaries

Before they request a quote, homeowners have questions, usually the same ones. If your website answers them clearly, you build trust, shorten sales calls and pick up search traffic for each question. If it doesn't, they'll find the answers on a competitor's site.

Here are the 25 to cover, grouped by theme.

Cost and savings

  1. How much do solar panels cost? Give typical ranges for common system sizes in your area.
  2. How much will I save? Explain what affects savings and link to your calculator.
  3. How long is the payback period? A typical range with a worked example.
  4. Are there grants or incentives? Summarise and link to your incentives page.
  5. Is finance available? Outline the options.
  6. Will solar increase my home's value? Share what's known honestly.

Suitability

  1. Is my roof suitable? Orientation, pitch, shading and condition.
  2. Does solar work in cloudy weather or winter? Yes, at reduced output; explain.
  3. How many panels do I need? Based on usage and roof space.
  4. Do I need planning permission? State local rules and exceptions.
  5. Can I install solar on a flat roof or the ground? Explain options.

How it works

  1. How do solar panels work? Two or three sentences and a diagram.
  2. What happens to electricity I don't use? Export payments or net metering in your region.
  3. Will solar power my home in a power cut? Only with a battery and the right inverter; be clear.
  4. Do I need a battery? Pros, cons and who benefits most.
  5. Can I charge an electric car with solar? Yes; link to your EV charger page.

Installation

  1. How long does installation take? Typical days on site and overall timeline.
  2. What's involved in the process? Survey, design, install, commissioning.
  3. Will there be disruption? Scaffolding, brief power-down, access needed.
  4. Who handles the paperwork? Grid applications, grants, certificates.

After installation

  1. How long do panels last? Typical lifespan and degradation.
  2. What warranties do I get? Product, performance and workmanship.
  3. Do panels need maintenance or cleaning? Minimal; explain.
  4. How do I monitor my system? App and what it shows.
  5. What if something goes wrong? Your aftercare and response times.

How to write good answers

  • Answer first. Put the direct answer in the first sentence.
  • Keep it short. Two to four sentences, then link to a detailed page.
  • Use local specifics. Your region's rules, rates and schemes.
  • Be honest. Admit limitations; it builds credibility.
  • Update regularly. Especially costs and incentives.

Where FAQs should live

  • A dedicated FAQ page, grouped by topic
  • The most relevant three to five on each service page
  • A few on the homepage
  • In area pages, for local questions

Mark them up with schema

FAQ schema tells search engines exactly which text is the question and which is the answer. That helps your content appear in rich results and makes it easier for AI assistants to quote you. See our schema markup guide and how to get mentioned in AI search.

Turn the big ones into articles

Questions like "how much do solar panels cost in [region]?" and "is solar worth it?" deserve full guides of their own. Link to them from the short FAQ answer. This is the backbone of SEO for solar companies.

Use them in sales

Send links to relevant answers after a call. It reinforces what you said and shows you're thorough.

Frequently asked questions

How many FAQs should a solar site have?

Start with these 25. Add more as your sales team hears new questions.

Should answers include prices?

Typical ranges, yes. They're among the most searched questions and avoiding them pushes visitors elsewhere.

Do FAQs help with SEO?

Yes. Each is a chance to match a specific search, and structured answers are favoured by both search engines and AI tools.
