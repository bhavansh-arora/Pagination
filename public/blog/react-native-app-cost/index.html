How Much Does a Mobile App Cost in 2026?

Realistic mobile app development costs in 2026, what drives the price, how React Native reduces it, ongoing costs and how to budget for an MVP.

Key takeaways
  • A focused MVP typically costs $15,000 to $40,000; complex apps cost far more
  • Features, design, backend and integrations drive cost more than screen count
  • React Native lets one codebase serve iOS and Android
  • Budget 15 to 20% of the build cost each year for maintenance

App pricing is confusing because "an app" can mean a simple booking tool or the next ride-sharing platform. The useful question isn't "how much does an app cost?" but "how much does this app cost, and what's the smallest version worth building first?"

Typical ranges

App typeTypical costTimeline
Simple MVP (few screens, basic features)$15,000 to $40,0006 to 12 weeks
Mid-complexity (accounts, payments, admin)$40,000 to $100,0003 to 6 months
Complex (real-time, marketplace, heavy integrations)$100,000 to $250,000+6 to 12 months+

Rates vary considerably by region and team. A senior studio in a lower-cost country can deliver the same quality for less than a large agency in a major city.

What drives the cost

1. Features

Each feature is a mini project. Common ones:

  • User accounts and login
  • Profiles and settings
  • Payments and subscriptions
  • Push notifications
  • Chat or messaging
  • Maps and location
  • Camera, scanning, media upload
  • Search and filters
  • Offline mode
  • Admin dashboard

2. Platforms

Building separate native apps for iOS and Android roughly doubles the work. Cross-platform frameworks avoid that.

3. Design

Custom, polished interfaces with animation take longer than standard components. Good design is worth it; it's what users judge first.

4. Backend

Most apps need a server: database, APIs, authentication, file storage. Often this is as much work as the app itself.

5. Integrations

Payment providers, CRMs, booking systems, mapping services, analytics.

6. Compliance and security

Health, finance or children's apps carry extra requirements.

How React Native reduces cost

React Native lets developers write one codebase that runs as a real app on both iOS and Android.

  • One team, one codebase
  • Faster development
  • Shared updates across platforms
  • Near-native performance for most apps

It's why it's our default for client apps. Fully native development still makes sense for some graphics-heavy or highly specialised apps.

Why maintenance matters

Operating systems update every year. Devices change. Libraries need patching. An app left alone slowly breaks. Budget for it from day one.

How to keep the budget under control

Build an MVP

A minimum viable product has only the features needed to deliver the core value. Launch it, learn from real users, then invest in what they actually use.

Prioritise ruthlessly

Sort features into must-have, should-have and later. Launch with the first list.

Use existing services

Authentication, payments, notifications and chat can be handled by proven services so you're not building them from scratch.

Prototype first

A clickable prototype costs little and catches expensive misunderstandings before development begins.

Choose cross-platform

Unless you have a specific reason not to.

Could a web app do the job?

Before committing, ask whether a web app or progressive web app would meet the need at lower cost. See do you need a mobile app or a website?

What a quote should include

  • Feature list with estimates
  • Design, development, testing and project management
  • Backend and admin panel
  • App store submission
  • Post-launch support period
  • Ownership of source code
  • Ongoing maintenance options

Questions to ask a developer

  1. Have you built similar apps? Can I try them?
  2. Who owns the code?
  3. What's included after launch?
  4. How do you handle changes to scope?
  5. What will running costs be?

Frequently asked questions

Can I build an app for under $10,000?

A very simple app or a prototype, possibly. A production-ready app with a backend usually costs more.

How long does an app take to build?

A focused MVP typically takes 6 to 12 weeks.
