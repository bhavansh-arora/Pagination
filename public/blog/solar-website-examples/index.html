Key takeaways
  • Strong solar sites lead with a clear benefit and an immediate quote path
  • Local identity and grant expertise are powerful differentiators
  • Calculators and incentive messaging do much of the selling
  • Each example adapts the same principles to its own market

Solar websites often look interchangeable: a blue sky, a roof, "Go solar today". These five, all designed and built by Code Bunny, show how a clear message and a distinct identity make an installer memorable. For each, we've highlighted the idea most worth borrowing.

1. Eco Horizon — Ireland

Solar PV, batteries and EV charging for Irish homes, businesses and farms. The headline, "Powering Ireland's solar future", is confident and national. Grants (SEAI and TAMS 3) are woven through the site, and a 5/5 rating from dozens of reviews sits near the top.

Borrow this: make grant handling part of your core promise.

2. SunFlow Solar & Exteriors — Barrie, Ontario

Solar, roofing and luxury exteriors under one brand. "Power from the roof up" ties the services together, and "100% Canadian" is a clear trust signal.

Borrow this: if you offer related services, unite them under one idea.

3. Lithium Solar Energy — Southern California

Solar, battery backup, panel upgrades and EV charging from a Yucaipa team with 17+ years in power. The "Go solar. Save big." hero is direct, and the end-to-end promise (designed, permitted, installed, monitored) reassures.

Borrow this: spell out that one team handles everything.

4. Watthaus Strategy — Auckland, New Zealand

Bridging real estate and renewable energy: rooftop solar for homes and land-to-construction for utility-scale farms. Two audiences, each given its own clear path.

Borrow this: split your homepage by audience when you serve very different buyers.
