Key takeaways
  • Patients need three things quickly: what you treat, who they'll see and how to book
  • Provider profiles with photos build trust before the first visit
  • Accessibility and privacy are essential, not optional
  • Keep health information accurate, reviewed and clearly dated

A clinic website is often visited by someone who's worried, in pain or looking after a family member. They need clear answers quickly. The best healthcare sites are calm, well organised and easy to use for people of all ages and abilities.

The first screen

  • A reassuring headline: "Healthcare that feels personal again"
  • A real photo of your team or clinic
  • "Book an appointment" and a phone number
  • Opening hours and location
  • An emergency notice telling people what to do in an urgent situation

Services

List what you offer, grouped sensibly: general practice, women's health, children's health, chronic disease management, vaccinations, minor procedures, mental health, allied health.

Give key services their own pages with:

  • What's involved
  • Who it's for
  • How to prepare
  • Fees or coverage
  • How to book

Provider profiles

For each clinician:

  • Professional photo
  • Qualifications and registration
  • Special interests
  • Languages spoken
  • Days available
  • A short personal note

Patients often choose a doctor from these pages.

Appointments

  • Online booking integrated with your practice system
  • Telehealth options, if available
  • Clear instructions for repeat prescriptions and results
  • New patient registration forms
  • Cancellation policy

More in online booking for small businesses.

Practical information

  • Address, parking, public transport and accessibility
  • Hours, including after-hours arrangements
  • Fees, insurance and billing
  • What to bring to a first appointment

Accessibility

Healthcare sites serve people who may be elderly, unwell or using assistive technology.

  • Large, readable type and strong contrast
  • Clear headings and simple navigation
  • Keyboard-friendly forms
  • Alt text on images
  • Plain language

See web accessibility basics.

Privacy and security

  • HTTPS across the site
  • Don't collect sensitive health details through basic contact forms
  • Use secure, compliant systems for bookings and patient information
  • A clear privacy policy
  • Follow the rules that apply in your country (HIPAA, GDPR, local health privacy law)

Health content

Articles and FAQs help patients and bring search traffic. Because health is a "your money or your life" topic, Google holds it to a high standard.

  • Have content written or reviewed by a clinician
  • Show the author and review date
  • Cite reputable sources
  • Avoid promising outcomes

Local SEO

  • Google Business Profile with categories, services and booking link
  • Reviews, within the bounds of your regulator's rules
  • Location in titles
  • MedicalClinic and Physician schema

See our local SEO guide.

Design

Calm colours, plenty of white space, genuine photography. Avoid clutter and pop-ups. Speed matters: many patients are on phones.

Frequently asked questions

Can patients book online through the website?

Yes, by integrating your practice management or booking system.

What should a clinic never put on its site?

Unverified health claims, patient information without consent, or forms that collect sensitive data insecurely.

How often should the site be updated?

Whenever providers, hours, fees or services change, with a full review at least twice a year.
