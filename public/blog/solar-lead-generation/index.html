Solar Lead Generation: 15 Ways to Get More Quote Requests

Fifteen proven ways for solar installers to generate qualified leads from their website, Google, reviews, referrals and content, without buying shared leads.

Key takeaways
  • Leads you generate yourself convert far better than bought, shared leads
  • Your website, Google Business Profile and reviews form the foundation
  • Calculators and incentive guides capture people still researching
  • Speed of follow-up often matters more than lead volume

Buying leads from aggregators feels easy until you notice the same homeowner was sold to four other installers. Exclusive leads from your own marketing cost less over time and close at much higher rates, because the customer chose you.

Here are fifteen ways to generate them.

Foundations

1. A website built to convert

Clear benefit, clear proof, clear next step. If your site doesn't have a quote button on every screen, start there. See how to build a solar company website.

2. A savings calculator

Give visitors a personalised estimate and ask for contact details to send the full breakdown. More on calculators.

3. A short, friendly quote form

Three to five fields. How to design it.

4. Google Business Profile

Fill in everything, add project photos weekly and gather reviews. This is what puts you in the map results. See our optimisation checklist.

5. Reviews, constantly

Ask every customer on commissioning day. A steady flow of recent reviews lifts rankings and trust.

Search

6. Local SEO

Service area pages, local keywords and schema markup. SEO for solar companies explains the plan.

7. Incentive and grant content

People search "solar grants [country]" and "is solar worth it in [region]" long before they request quotes. A clear, current guide captures them early. How to write it.

8. FAQ content

Answer the questions homeowners type into Google. 25 to start with.

9. Google Ads on high-intent keywords

"Solar installers near me" and "solar panel quote [town]" are expensive but valuable. Send clicks to a focused landing page, not your homepage. Compare the trade-offs in SEO vs Google Ads.

Proof and referrals

10. Case studies

Short write-ups with photos, system size, cost and savings. "A 6 kW system in Barrie saving $1,900 a year" is more convincing than any claim.

11. A referral programme

Happy customers have neighbours with identical roofs. Offer a thank-you for referrals and make it easy to share.

12. Yard signs and neighbour letters

Old-fashioned and effective. Add a QR code to a landing page for that street or town.

Content and social

13. Project photos and video

Time-lapse installs, drone shots and customer interviews perform well on Facebook, Instagram and YouTube and reassure people that you do real work locally.

14. Email nurture

Most leads aren't ready immediately. A short series (how solar works, incentives, a case study, financing) keeps you front of mind.

15. Partnerships

Builders, roofers, electricians, EV dealers and estate agents all meet homeowners at decision points. Offer a simple referral arrangement.

Don't waste the leads you get

Lead generation is only half the job.

  • Respond within minutes during business hours
  • Call, then text, then email
  • Follow up at least five times over two weeks
  • Track every lead in a CRM with its source

Installers who respond in five minutes win far more often than those who respond the next day.

Frequently asked questions

Are bought solar leads worth it?

They can fill gaps, but shared leads are contacted by several installers and close at low rates. Building your own pipeline pays back better.

How many leads does a website generate?

It depends on traffic and conversion rate. A well-built site typically converts 3 to 8% of visitors into enquiries.

What's the fastest way to get more leads?

Fix your website's quote path and response time, then turn on tightly targeted Google Ads while SEO builds.
