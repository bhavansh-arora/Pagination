AI Chatbots for Small Business: What They Can (and Can't) Do

A practical guide to AI chatbots for small businesses: real use cases, costs, how they're trained on your content, pitfalls and how to add one to your website.

Key takeaways
  • A good chatbot answers common questions instantly, captures leads and books appointments around the clock
  • It should be trained on your own content and hand over to a human when unsure
  • Start with one clear job, such as FAQs or lead capture
  • Always tell visitors they're talking to an AI

Customers ask the same questions again and again: what are your prices, are you open Saturday, do you cover my area, can I book for Thursday? An AI chatbot can answer those instantly, at 2am, in several languages, and pass the serious enquiries to you with the details already collected.

We build chatbots and run our own chatbot product, CustomBotify. Here's a clear-eyed look at what they do well.

What a modern AI chatbot is

Earlier chatbots followed rigid scripts: press 1 for hours. Today's are powered by large language models and can understand natural questions, then answer from a knowledge base you provide: your website, FAQs, price list, policies and documents.

What they're good at

Answering common questions

Hours, prices, services, policies, delivery, how things work.

Capturing leads

Asking for a name, email and what the visitor needs, then sending it to your inbox or CRM.

Qualifying enquiries

"What's your budget? When do you need it? Where are you based?" so you spend time on the right ones.

Booking

Connecting to your calendar to offer available times.

Supporting customers

Order status, how-to help, returns.

Working across channels

The same assistant on your website, WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger.

What they're not good at

  • Judgement calls on complex or sensitive matters
  • Anything they haven't been given information about
  • Replacing human relationships in high-trust sales
  • Regulated advice (medical, legal, financial) without strict controls

A good chatbot knows its limits and hands over gracefully.

How it learns about your business

  1. You supply content: website pages, documents, FAQs
  2. The system indexes it so the AI can retrieve relevant passages
  3. When asked a question, it finds the right information and writes an answer based on it
  4. You refine: review conversations, fix gaps, add content

This approach keeps answers grounded in your material and makes updates easy: change the document and the bot's answers change too.

Guardrails that matter

  • Stick to your content. Instruct the bot to say "I'm not sure, let me connect you with the team" when it doesn't know.
  • Disclose that it's AI. Honesty builds trust and is increasingly a legal requirement.
  • Offer a human handover: email, phone, WhatsApp or live chat.
  • Protect privacy: don't collect sensitive data in chat, and mention the chatbot in your privacy policy.
  • Review transcripts regularly, especially at first.

Costs

OptionTypical cost
No-code chatbot platform$20 to $150 a month
Custom-built assistant with integrations$1,500 to $10,000+ to build, plus usage
AI usage feesUsually cents per conversation

For most small businesses a no-code tool is the right starting point.

Where to start

Pick one job:

  1. FAQ assistant: deflect repetitive questions
  2. Lead capture: engage visitors who'd otherwise leave
  3. Booking helper: turn interest into appointments

Launch, watch real conversations for two weeks, improve, then expand.

Adding it to your site

  • A chat bubble in the corner, loaded after the page so it doesn't slow things down
  • A welcome message that says what it can help with
  • Suggested questions to get people started
  • A clear route to a human

Mind the placement: don't cover your call-to-action buttons or WhatsApp link on mobile.

Measuring success

  • Conversations started
  • Questions answered without human help
  • Leads captured
  • Bookings made
  • Customer satisfaction

Frequently asked questions

Will a chatbot annoy my customers?

Not if it's helpful, honest about being AI and easy to bypass for a human.

Can it speak other languages?

Yes. Modern models handle many languages well, which is valuable for diverse customer bases.

Is my data safe?

Choose providers with clear data policies, avoid collecting sensitive information in chat and check where data is stored.
