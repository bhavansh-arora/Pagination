FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still curious? Ask us anything

How much does a website cost?

Every project is quoted on scope. A focused one-page site costs far less than a multi-page site with booking, payments or a custom dashboard. You get a fixed, written price before any work starts, and the free preview lets you see the quality before you spend anything.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most landing pages and small business sites go live in 1–2 weeks. Larger multi-page builds, e-commerce stores and web apps are scoped individually, and you'll get a clear timeline with your quote.

Will my website rank on Google?

Every Code Bunny site ships with technical SEO built in: fast load times, clean semantic HTML, structured data (schema), optimised titles and meta descriptions, an XML sitemap and mobile-first layouts. We can also help with ongoing local SEO and content.

Do I own my website?

Yes, 100%. You get the full source code, design files and admin access. No lock-in, no hostage hosting. Host it with us or anywhere you like.

Want to see your new website before you pay?

Tell us about your business and we'll design a live homepage preview, free, usually within 3–5 business days. No card. No contract. No pressure.
