About Code Bunny

A small studio with a big-agency standard.

Code Bunny is a founder-led web design and development studio. For 5 years we've designed, built and shipped websites, apps and software for founders and small businesses around the world.

Hi, I'm Bhavansh.

I founded Code Bunny with a simple belief: small businesses deserve websites as good as the big brands', without the big-agency price tag, the slow handoffs or the account manager who's never seen the code.

Code Bunny is part studio, part product lab. Alongside client work we build and run our own products: CustomBotify, a no-code AI chatbot builder, and live sites like Thrill Lands and LaunchMint. Building our own products keeps us honest: we know what it takes to make a site that actually earns money.

Today we've shipped 100+ projects for coaches, wedding photographers and filmmakers, solar installers, clinics, schools and startups in United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand and India.

And because we're confident in our work, we'll design your homepage for free before you commit.
