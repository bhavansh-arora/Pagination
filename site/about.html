Our story

From childhood memories to changing lives.

Mark Jones started MDJ with a simple idea: mortgage advice should feel personal, because a home is personal.

Some of Mark’s earliest memories are of visits to his grandmother’s home in Farnham, a place full of family, warmth and security. That feeling of what a home means to a family is what drives the business today, whether he’s helping a first-time buyer leave renting behind or advising a retired couple on releasing equity to help their grandchildren.

MDJ has grown to a small, close-knit team with an outsized reputation, recognised in the Quality Business Awards three years running and trusted by hundreds of families across Hampshire, Berkshire and Surrey.
