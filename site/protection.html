Mortgage & family protection

Protect the home you worked for

Your mortgage is probably your biggest monthly commitment. Protection makes sure it's still paid if you can't work, become seriously ill or aren't here, so your family can stay put.

Types of cover

Cover that fits your life

We'll recommend what you actually need and nothing you don't, then check it still fits each time your circumstances change.

Life insurance

Pays a lump sum if you die during the policy term, to clear the mortgage or support your family.

Critical illness cover

A tax-free lump sum if you're diagnosed with a specified serious illness, such as cancer, heart attack or stroke.

Income protection

A monthly income if illness or injury stops you working, paid until you return or the policy ends.

Home insurance

Buildings and contents cover, which most lenders require from the day you exchange.

Family income benefit

A regular, tax-free income for your family rather than a single lump sum, often at a lower cost.

Business protection

Key person and shareholder cover for directors and business owners.

Already have cover?

When did you last review it?

Policies taken out years ago may no longer match your mortgage, your family or your health. A review when you remortgage can close gaps, and sometimes cut your premiums.
