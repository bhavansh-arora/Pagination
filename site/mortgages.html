02 · Moving home

Moving up, moving on.

Talk to an adviser

Whether you need more space, a better school catchment or a fresh start, we’ll arrange the finance so your move goes smoothly and your chain keeps moving.

When you move you can often port your existing mortgage, borrow extra from your current lender, or take a brand-new deal. The right choice depends on early repayment charges, the rates available and how much more you need. We compare all three for you.

  • Porting vs. switching: we calculate the true cost of each
  • Bridging and chain-break options if timing is tight
  • Agreements in principle so you can act fast on the right property
  • We liaise with your agent and solicitor to keep things on track

03 · Remortgaging

Don’t drift onto the SVR.

Talk to an adviser

When a fixed or tracker rate ends, most lenders move you onto their Standard Variable Rate, which is often much more expensive. We start reviewing your options up to six months early so you’re ready to switch.

Remortgaging can lower your monthly payments, release equity for home improvements, consolidate debt or simply give you certainty with a new fix. We compare a product transfer with your current lender against the whole market, so you know you’re getting the best option, not just the easiest one.

  • Reviews up to six months before your deal ends
  • Product transfers vs. the whole of market, compared side by side
  • Capital raising for extensions, renovations or a family gift
  • A protection check-up: life cover when remortgaging could cost less than you think

Questions

Mortgage FAQs

How big a deposit do I need?

Most lenders need at least 5% of the purchase price. A 10% or 15% deposit opens up more lenders and lower rates. Some specialist products, such as guarantor or family-assisted mortgages, may need less of your own money.

How long does a mortgage application take?

From application to offer usually takes two to four weeks, depending on the lender and how quickly the valuation happens. Having your documents ready speeds things up, and we’ll give you a checklist.

Will checking my options affect my credit score?

Our initial research doesn’t affect your score. Many lenders now use soft searches for a Decision in Principle, and we’ll always tell you before any hard search.

Fixed or tracker: which is better?

A fixed rate gives you certainty, while a tracker follows the Bank of England base rate. The right choice depends on your budget, plans and attitude to risk. We’ll go through the options with you.
