---
Choosing a dentist is personal, and for many people a bit daunting. A new patient visiting your website is asking: will I be looked after, can I afford it, and how do I book? Answer those three questions clearly and you'll convert far more visitors than a site full of clinical jargon.

## Reassurance comes first

- **Warm, real photography** of your team and practice
- **Friendly language:** "nervous patients welcome", "we'll go at your pace"
- **A clean, bright design** that feels modern and hygienic
- **Clear information** about what happens at a first visit

## Make booking easy

- A "Book appointment" button in the header and repeated down the page
- Online booking if your practice system supports it; otherwise a short request form
- A tappable phone number
- Emergency appointment information clearly visible
- Opening hours, including evenings and weekends

## Treatment pages

Give each main treatment its own page. Patients search by treatment: "teeth whitening [town]", "dental implants cost", "Invisalign near me".

On each page:

- What the treatment is, in plain English
- Who it's for
- What to expect, step by step
- How long it takes
- Fees or a guide price
- Before-and-after photos (with consent)
- FAQs
- A booking button

Typical pages: check-ups, hygiene, whitening, orthodontics and aligners, implants, veneers, crowns, emergency care, children's dentistry.

## Fees and payment

Publish a fee guide. Explain insurance, plans and finance options. Transparency removes one of the biggest barriers to booking.

## Meet the team

A photo, qualifications, registration number, special interests and a line of personality for each clinician. Patients like knowing who'll be treating them.

## Reviews

Display your Google rating and a selection of reviews. Stories from nervous patients who had a good experience are especially persuasive.

[[work:tpl-dentist,tpl-doctor]]

## New patient section

A dedicated page covering:

- How to register
- What happens at the first appointment
- Forms to complete in advance
- Offers for new patients
- Parking, access and directions

[[cta]]

## Local SEO for dentists

- A complete Google Business Profile with the right categories ([checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization/))
- Reviews, requested consistently
- Location in page titles
- Treatment pages targeting "[treatment] [town]"
- Dentist and LocalBusiness schema ([guide](/blog/schema-markup-guide/))

See the full [local SEO guide](/blog/local-seo-guide/).

## Compliance

Dental marketing is regulated. Check your regulator's rules on:

- Displaying registration numbers
- Advertising claims and before-and-after images
- Patient testimonials
- Privacy and consent for forms

When in doubt, ask your indemnity provider.

## Accessibility

Many patients are older or have accessibility needs. Use readable text sizes, good colour contrast and clear navigation. See [web accessibility basics](/blog/website-accessibility-basics/).

## A ready-made option

Our Dentava dental design is a bright, reassuring practice site with a smile gallery and new-patient booking, available on the [templates page](/templates/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Should a dental website show prices?
Yes. A fee guide builds trust and reduces enquiries from people outside your price range.

### Do we need online booking?
It's increasingly expected. If your system doesn't support it, a simple appointment request form is the next best thing.

### How do dental practices get more new patients online?
A fast, reassuring website, strong treatment pages, an active Google Business Profile and a steady flow of reviews.

=== medical-clinic-website
title: Medical Clinic Website Guide: Trust, Access and Appointments
desc: How to build a medical clinic website patients trust: clear services, provider profiles, appointment booking, accessibility and privacy considerations.
cat: local
kw: medical clinic website, healthcare website design, doctor website, medical practice website
takeaways: Patients need three things quickly: what you treat, who they'll see and how to book | Provider profiles with photos build trust before the first visit | Accessibility and privacy are essential, not optional | Keep health information accurate, reviewed and clearly dated
---
A clinic website is often visited by someone who's worried, in pain or looking after a family member. They need clear answers quickly. The best healthcare sites are calm, well organised and easy to use for people of all ages and abilities.

## The first screen

- A reassuring headline: "Healthcare that feels personal again"
- A real photo of your team or clinic
- "Book an appointment" and a phone number
- Opening hours and location
- An emergency notice telling people what to do in an urgent situation

## Services

List what you offer, grouped sensibly: general practice, women's health, children's health, chronic disease management, vaccinations, minor procedures, mental health, allied health.

Give key services their own pages with:

- What's involved
- Who it's for
- How to prepare
- Fees or coverage
- How to book

## Provider profiles

For each clinician:

- Professional photo
- Qualifications and registration
- Special interests
- Languages spoken
- Days available
- A short personal note

Patients often choose a doctor from these pages.

## Appointments

- Online booking integrated with your practice system
- Telehealth options, if available
- Clear instructions for repeat prescriptions and results
- New patient registration forms
- Cancellation policy

More in [online booking for small businesses](/blog/online-booking-for-small-business/).

## Practical information

- Address, parking, public transport and accessibility
- Hours, including after-hours arrangements
- Fees, insurance and billing
- What to bring to a first appointment

[[work:tpl-doctor,tpl-dentist]]

## Accessibility

Healthcare sites serve people who may be elderly, unwell or using assistive technology.

- Large, readable type and strong contrast
- Clear headings and simple navigation
- Keyboard-friendly forms
- Alt text on images
- Plain language

See [web accessibility basics](/blog/website-accessibility-basics/).

## Privacy and security

- HTTPS across the site
- Don't collect sensitive health details through basic contact forms
- Use secure, compliant systems for bookings and patient information
- A clear privacy policy
- Follow the rules that apply in your country (HIPAA, GDPR, local health privacy law)

[[cta]]

## Health content

Articles and FAQs help patients and bring search traffic. Because health is a "your money or your life" topic, Google holds it to a high standard.

- Have content written or reviewed by a clinician
- Show the author and review date
- Cite reputable sources
- Avoid promising outcomes

## Local SEO

- Google Business Profile with categories, services and booking link
- Reviews, within the bounds of your regulator's rules
- Location in titles
- MedicalClinic and Physician schema

See our [local SEO guide](/blog/local-seo-guide/).

## Design

Calm colours, plenty of white space, genuine photography. Avoid clutter and pop-ups. Speed matters: many patients are on phones.

## A ready-made option

Our Meridian Care Clinic design is a trust-building practice site with provider bios, services and appointment requests, available on the [templates page](/templates/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can patients book online through the website?
Yes, by integrating your practice management or booking system.

### What should a clinic never put on its site?
Unverified health claims, patient information without consent, or forms that collect sensitive data insecurely.

### How often should the site be updated?
Whenever providers, hours, fees or services change, with a full review at least twice a year.

---
People looking for a mortgage are anxious about the biggest financial commitment of their lives. They compare several brokers and choose the one who seems most trustworthy and easiest to deal with. Your website's job is to be that broker before they've spoken to you.

## Lead with trust

- **Review rating and count** near the top: "5.0 on Google from 186 reviews"
- **Awards and accreditations**
- **Real photos** of the advisers
- **Regulatory details** clearly displayed
- **A friendly, human headline**

MDJ Mortgages does this well: "The mortgage advice Basingstoke trusts, from people who pick up the phone."

[[work:mdj-mortgages,tpl-doctor]]

## Calculators

Calculators are the most engaging feature on a mortgage site.

- **How much could I borrow?**
- **Monthly repayment**
- **Stamp duty or closing costs**
- **Remortgage savings**

Show an instant indicative result, then offer a personalised figure from an adviser. It's value first, lead capture second.

## A page for each client type

Borrowers search for their own situation.

- First-time buyers
- Home movers
- Remortgage / refinance
- Self-employed and contractors
- Buy-to-let / investment
- Later-life lending
- Poor credit
- Protection and insurance

Each page should explain the process, common worries, what documents are needed and include a relevant client story.

## Explain the process

Three or four steps from first chat to keys in hand. Reassure visitors that the first conversation is free and without obligation.

## Make contact easy

- Book a call (embedded calendar)
- Request a callback
- WhatsApp
- Phone
- A short enquiry form

Different people prefer different routes. Offer several. See [adding WhatsApp to your website](/blog/whatsapp-for-business-website/).

[[cta]]

## Fees

Be upfront about how you're paid: fee-free, a fixed fee or a percentage. Transparency is both a regulatory requirement in many places and a trust builder.

## Content that brings in borrowers

- First-time buyer guides
- "How much deposit do I need?"
- "Can I get a mortgage if I'm self-employed?"
- "Fixed vs variable rates"
- Local property market updates

Helpful guides rank on Google and position you as the expert. See [how to write a blog post that ranks](/blog/how-to-write-a-blog-post-that-ranks/).

## Compliance

Financial promotions are regulated.

- Include required risk warnings ("Your home may be repossessed...")
- Display your regulatory status and firm reference number
- Avoid guarantees
- Keep rate examples current and representative
- Have content approved through your compliance process

## Local SEO

- "Mortgage broker [town]" in titles and headings
- Google Business Profile with reviews ([checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization/))
- Local landing pages for nearby towns ([how](/blog/local-landing-pages/))
- FinancialService schema

## Design

Financial sites don't need to be dull. A warm, premium design with clear typography stands out against competitors' generic templates and signals competence.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do calculators need to be exact?
They should be indicative and clearly labelled as such, with an invitation to get a personalised figure.

### Should brokers show rates on their site?
Only if you can keep them current and compliant. Many prefer to explain that rates depend on circumstances.

### What converts best on a broker website?
Strong reviews, a useful calculator and an easy way to book a free call.

=== restaurant-website-guide
title: Restaurant Website Must-Haves: Menus, Bookings and Local Search
desc: What every restaurant website needs: a readable menu, online reservations and ordering, great photos, hours, location and the local SEO to be found.
cat: local
kw: restaurant website, restaurant website design, restaurant menu online, cafe website
takeaways: Diners want four things fast: menu, hours, location and a way to book or order | Publish the menu as text, not only a PDF | Take direct bookings and orders to avoid high platform commissions | Keep your Google Business Profile and hours accurate at all times
---
Someone's hungry. They open your website on their phone. They want to know what you serve, whether you're open, where you are and how to book. If they can't find all four within seconds, they'll pick another restaurant.

## The essentials, above everything

1. **Menu**
2. **Hours**
3. **Address** with a tap-to-navigate map link
4. **Phone**, tappable
5. **Book a table / Order online**

Put these on the homepage and in the header or footer of every page.

## The menu

- **Use real text,** not just a PDF or image. Text loads fast, is readable on phones, works with screen readers and can be indexed by Google.
- **Keep prices current.**
- **Mark dietary options:** vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, allergens.
- **Organise clearly** by course or category.
- **Offer a PDF as a download** if you like, alongside the text version.

## Reservations

- Embed your booking system (OpenTable, Resy, SevenRooms, ResDiary or a simple form)
- Show a "Book a table" button everywhere
- Allow notes for allergies and occasions
- Send confirmation and reminder messages

## Online ordering

Third-party delivery apps bring reach and take a large commission. Direct ordering through your own site keeps the margin and the customer relationship.

- Offer pickup and delivery through your own system where practical
- Link clearly to it: "Order direct"
- Still list delivery apps if you use them

## Photography

Food sells through pictures.

- Invest in a short professional shoot
- Show dishes, the room and the people
- Compress images so the site stays fast ([image SEO](/blog/image-seo/))
- Avoid stock food photos

## Tell your story

A short about section: who you are, what you cook, where ingredients come from. People love to know.

## Extras worth adding

- Private dining and events enquiries
- Gift vouchers
- Catering menu
- Upcoming events
- Newsletter sign-up
- Press and awards

[[cta]]

## Local search

Most diners find restaurants through Google Maps.

- Keep your Google Business Profile complete: menu link, booking link, photos, attributes ([checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization/))
- Update hours for holidays
- Reply to reviews
- Add Restaurant and Menu schema ([guide](/blog/schema-markup-guide/))

See the full [local SEO guide](/blog/local-seo-guide/).

## Mobile and speed

Nearly all restaurant browsing is on phones. Design for a thumb, test on mobile data and skip auto-playing music or heavy intro animations.

## Common mistakes

- Menu only as a huge PDF
- Out-of-date hours
- No prices
- Music that plays automatically
- A site that only works on desktop
- Hidden address

## Catering and food manufacturing

If you supply equipment or cater commercially, a catalog-style site works well. IB Refrigeration, for example, presents display counters, food carts and commercial kitchen equipment as a browsable product range.

[[work:ibrefrigeration,tpl-event]]

## Frequently asked questions

### Do restaurants still need a website with Instagram and delivery apps?
Yes. A website is the one place you fully control, and it's what Google links to from your profile.

### How often should the menu be updated?
Whenever dishes or prices change. An out-of-date menu causes disappointment and bad reviews.

### Can I take orders without paying commission?
Yes, with a direct ordering system on your own site, paying only payment processing fees.
