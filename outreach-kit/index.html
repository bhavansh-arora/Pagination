Step one

Find businesses to contact

Search Google Maps for a business type in your area. Open each listing, tap the website link on your phone, and keep the ones where you can name a problem in under a minute.

Worth contacting

  • At least 4 stars and 25+ Google reviews. They're busy and can afford a site.
  • One new customer is worth a lot to them: dentists, lawyers, roofers, med spas, contractors.
  • The site has a problem you can see on a phone in under a minute.
  • The owner or manager's name is on the site, Google listing or LinkedIn.
  • They run Google ads or post on Instagram. They already spend on marketing.

Skip these

  • Franchises and chains. The website is controlled by head office.
  • Businesses with fewer than 10 reviews or no recent activity.
  • Sites that are already fast, modern and mobile-friendly. You have nothing specific to say.
  • Anyone whose only contact option is a generic web form with no name.
Make it easy to say yes

Offers that get replies

A free, specific thing gets far more replies than "I build websites." Pick one to lead with, then offer a package with a fixed price once they're talking to you.

OfferWhat you sendWhy it works
2-minute video auditA screen recording (Loom or similar) of you scrolling their site on a phone and pointing out three fixes.Shows you looked at their business in particular. Easy for them to watch and forward to a partner.
Homepage mockupOne image of what their homepage could look like, using their logo and photos.They can see the result before spending anything. Save it for the prospects who fit best, because it takes an hour or two.
Speed and SEO reportScreenshots of their PageSpeed Insights score and a search where a competitor ranks above them.Uses numbers from Google, which owners trust more than your opinion.
Fixed-price starter siteA clear package, for example a 5-page site built in two weeks with one price and one round of changes.Removes the fear of an open-ended bill. Offer it after they reply, not in the first email.
Keep going

A weekly routine

Outreach works through volume and follow-up. Most replies come after the second or third message, so send every step of the sequence.

MondayFind 25 prospects and note one problem for each.
TuesdaySend 10 first emails. Record a video for anyone who replies.
WednesdaySend 10 more first emails. Send the day-3 follow-ups.
ThursdayDM or call the prospects who didn't open your emails.
FridaySend day-7 and day-14 emails. Post one before-and-after on your socials.
