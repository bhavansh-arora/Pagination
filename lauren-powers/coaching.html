Questions

Before you apply.

Do I need to be in shape to start?

Not at all. Lauren started her own transformation from rock bottom. Every program begins with where you are today and is designed around your body, age and goals.

Is this only for women who want to compete?

No. Some clients want to step on stage, but most simply want to feel strong, confident and camera-ready in everyday life. Lauren designs for both.

I’m over 50. Is it too late?

Lauren won heavyweight titles into her mid-50s and is camera-ready in her 60s. Your age isn’t a limit. It’s your advantage.

How does the Brand & Bank side work?

Alongside training, you get coaching on presence, personal brand and an abundance mindset, plus practical guidance on turning your story and skills into income.

Is faith part of the coaching?

Faith is at the heart of Lauren’s story and is woven into her mindset coaching. Women of every background are welcome.
