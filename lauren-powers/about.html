Her story

The legend of
Lauren Powers.

Firefighter. Champion. Actress. Author. Mentor. Every arena she stepped into, she conquered. Here is the story behind the muscle.

10× CHAMPION · Hollywood’s strongest woman · International best-seller · CHAMPION MAKER

Beneath the Muscle

She didn’t start fierce.
She got there.

Firefighter. Addiction survivor. Heavyweight champion at an age most athletes retire. Then Hollywood came calling.

The Roots

Valedictorian, surfer, entrepreneur

Raised in Southern California, Lauren graduated valedictorian from Huntington Beach High, then ran her own surf academy on Maui while earning a business degree at the University of Hawaiʻi.

The Fire

Pushed out, not put out

She served as a firefighter and engineer until discrimination forced her out. She fought back in court and turned that fight into fuel for a whole new life.

The Fight

Rising above addiction

She beat drugs and alcohol, rebuilt from the inside out, and found her purpose in iron, discipline and faith.

The Crown

Ten-time champion

From 2002 to 2014 she took heavyweight and overall titles from California State to Muscle Beach Venice, competing and winning into her mid-50s.

The Spotlight

Screen, stage & story

HBO, E!, MSNBC and TLC, films with Seth Rogen and Kacey Musgraves, and music videos for Lady Gaga, J.Lo and Justin Bieber. Then came a best-selling book that reached readers in 11 countries.

The Mission

Making champions

Today Lauren pours everything she has learned into other women, helping them become strong, seen and financially free at any age.
