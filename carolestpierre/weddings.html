    (06) — Bridal FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Something else on your mind? Call us at 450 455-7999.

    01When should we book?

    As soon as your date is set. Wedding season fills quickly, and booking early lets us plan your trial and reserve the right team for your party.

    02Do you really come to us?

    Yes. Our Beauty in Action team travels to your home, hotel or venue within 15 km, for a minimum of 4 people, for hair, makeup or both. Beyond 15 km we add a $100 travel fee for the team.

    03Is a trial necessary?

    We highly recommend it. Hair and makeup trials take place at the salon and let you confirm every choice before the big day.

    04What if we need to start very early?

    We can. Services starting between 7:00 and 8:00 AM include a $25 supplement. Use the quote builder to see a suggested start time for your party.

    05I have very long or thick hair. Does it change the price?

    Extra-long hair adds $25. Ranges on the menu also reflect length, thickness and the stylist's experience level.

    06Can you take care of the groom and the kids?

    Of course. Groom hair or makeup is $45, and flower girls (12 and under) are $55 for hair and $35 for makeup in the salon.
