Before you book

  • Not during pregnancy or breastfeeding, or within 6 months of giving birth.
  • Wait 12 days after your last antibiotic, and 6 months after Accutane.
  • No sun, tanning beds or self-tanner for 2 weeks before.
  • No tattoos, skin lesions, infection or herpes on the treated area.
