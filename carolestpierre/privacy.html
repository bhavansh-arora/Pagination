Home/Privacy

Your privacy,respected.

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

When you use a form on this site (contact, bridal inquiry, newsletter) we receive only what you type: typically your name, email, phone number, birthday (day and month, for your birthday offer) and your message. Online booking and gift cards are handled by our booking provider, Zenoti, under its own privacy policy.

Why we collect it

To answer you, plan your appointments and, if you subscribed, send you our promotions. We never sell or rent your personal information.

Cookies

This site doesn't use advertising cookies. Your browser may store small preferences, such as whether the intro animation has already played, which never leave your device. The map on our Visit page is provided by Google Maps, which may set its own cookies when it loads.

Your rights

Under Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25), you may access, correct or ask us to delete your information, or withdraw your consent at any time.

Person in charge

Carole St-Pierre, President, is responsible for the protection of personal information. Write to info@carolest-pierre.com, call 450 455-7999, or visit us at 3210, boul. de la Gare, suite 170, Vaudreuil-Dorion, QC.
