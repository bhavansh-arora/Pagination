It's always been about you.

Dear guest,

It's always been very important for me to offer you the best. You deserve it.

The finest team of hair stylists, estheticians, massage therapists, manicurists and laser specialists, paired with the most refined selection of organic products for your face, body and hair.

Your beauty and wellness are our number-one priority, and my team and I are passionate about giving the attentive care that embodies our mission. Come discover the services and unique sensory experiences of our Aveda Concept salon and eco-responsible spa.

(02) — A pioneer

45 years.One motto.

Carole is a true pioneer of beauty, wellness and business, with more than 45 years of experience. Now semi-retired, she still practises the medical-esthetic treatments she cherishes, always first to embrace what's new.

“Give to the next.”

Her motto is the generous, caring approach she brings to every guest and every member of a team she has grown to more than 35 professionals, led day to day by General Manager Julie, who has grown alongside her for 30 years.
