05 — Spray tan

Sunless glow

Spray tan

$60

An Italian, anti-aging formula with Matrixyl that reaches the second layer of skin, so it fades evenly without blotches.

Spray tan express Fast

$65

Rinse in 1 to 4 hours depending on your desired depth. Keeps developing for 24 hours.

The night before

  • Exfoliate. Our coffee scrub gives the best results.
  • Shave, then lightly apply a water-based moisturiser.
  • Wax at least 24 hours before.
  • Come without makeup or perfume, in dark, loose clothes.

After your tan

  • No sweat or water for 8 to 12 hours.
  • First shower: rinse only, then pat dry.
  • Skip exfoliants and moisturise daily.
  • Enjoy a golden glow for 5 to 10 days.
