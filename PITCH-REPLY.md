Your current site already looks good. What we focused on is what happens *after* someone lands on it: getting the right commercial clients to press play, trust you, and start an enquiry. Here's what's different:

**1. Your brand films lead the site.**
Mary Lee's House, Fun Bike Center, TECO and The Dance Room now open the homepage in a cinematic reel. Your own film plays behind the headline, and every film plays in one click without leaving the page.

**2. Every film has its own case-study page.**
Each one covers the brief, your approach, who it was made for, and where it runs (web, Reels, showroom screens). Prospects stop seeing "a nice video" and start seeing a process they can buy. These pages also give you four new pages Google can rank for searches like "commercial video production Tampa".

**3. A "Film Finder" for undecided clients.**
Three quick questions recommend the right format: brand anthem, product showcase, social ad campaign or field feature. Each answer links to your matching case study, then carries over into the enquiry form.

**4. A Brief Builder instead of a blank contact form.**
Enquiries now come in pre-qualified with project type, goal, timeline and budget range. You can quote faster and skip the back-and-forth emails.

**5. Commercial and weddings, clearly separated.**
Brands go straight to the films. Couples get their own wedding page with your gallery, film stories and booking steps. Sports and product photography sit in a filterable stills gallery.

**6. Built to convert on phones.**
It's fully responsive across mobile, tablet and desktop, with a sticky "Call / Start a project" bar on phones. It also includes local-business structured data for Tampa Bay search, and one consistent reply-time promise (your current site says 48 hours on one page and one business day on another).

Everything uses your real work, copy and contact details. I'd love to walk you through it on a quick 15-minute call this week. Does Thursday or Friday work?
