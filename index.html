FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still curious? Ask us anything or call (800) 555-0199.

How far in advance should we book?

For weddings and large events, 9–14 months is ideal. Corporate events typically need 3–6 months. We've also pulled off stunning events in 3 weeks, so reach out even if your date is close.

Do you travel for destination events?

Absolutely. We've produced events in 22 countries. Our Atelier package covers guest travel, local permits and on-the-ground vendor management.

How does your pricing work?

We charge a transparent fixed planning fee, agreed up front. Vendor costs are passed through at our negotiated net rates with no hidden markups. You see every line item in your client portal.

Can we use our own vendors?

Of course. We happily collaborate with vendors you love and can fill any gaps from our network of 350+ vetted partners.

What if something goes wrong on the day?

Our on-site crew carries a contingency kit and backup plans for weather, power, timing and vendor no-shows. Our Zero-Stress Guarantee also stands behind every Signature and Atelier event.
