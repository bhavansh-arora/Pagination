FAQ

Questions, answered

Do you accept dental insurance?
Yes — we're in-network with most major PPO providers and offer in-house financing plans for treatments not covered by insurance.
How fast can I get an appointment?
Most new patients are seen within 3 business days, and we hold same-day slots open every day for dental emergencies.
Is Invisalign really as effective as braces?
For most cases, yes. Our specialists use 3D digital scanning to plan tooth movement precisely, achieving results comparable to traditional braces with far more comfort and discretion.
Do you treat anxious or first-time patients?
Absolutely — it's one of our specialties. We offer sedation options, noise-cancelling headphones, and a calm, spa-like studio designed to ease dental anxiety.
