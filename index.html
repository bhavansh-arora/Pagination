    Questions, answered

    Everything you're wondering.

    How far in advance should we book?
    For weddings and large-scale events we recommend 9–14 months. Corporate events and private celebrations can often be produced in 6–12 weeks — and we've delivered spectacular events in under 3 weeks when needed.
    Do you work with a fixed budget?
    Absolutely. We design to your number, not around it. You get a line-by-line transparent budget from day one, and we routinely save clients 15–25% through our preferred vendor network.
    Do you travel for destination events?
    Yes — we've produced events in 42 cities across 18 countries. Our team handles venues, permits, local vendors, guest travel and accommodation end-to-end.
    What happens if something goes wrong on the day?
    Every event comes with a written contingency plan, a backup for every critical vendor and an on-site operations lead whose only job is to solve problems before you ever notice them.
    Can we start with just a consultation?
    Of course. Your first 30-minute strategy call is completely free with no obligation. You'll leave with ideas you can use even if you don't hire us.
