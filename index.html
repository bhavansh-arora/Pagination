Hello, friend

That's me, Dan.

I take photos, I film — and sometimes I do both at the same time.

I have a passion for capturing images that are true to the moments as they happen. We take a documentary approach to photo and film: giving guidance when it's needed, but being careful to let your day flow naturally.

I love coffee, and I love diving into the latest photo and video tools that let me deliver stunning images and films. My wife Lydia and I always work together on weddings and large events. We both love travel, delicious food made with fresh ingredients — and cats.
