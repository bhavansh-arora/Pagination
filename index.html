Who we are

A boutique studio, obsessed with the details that make a house feel like home.

Atelier Interiors is a small team of designers who believe good design is felt, not just seen — the way morning light lands on a reading chair, the sound a door makes, the ease of a kitchen that works. We take on a limited number of projects each season so every client gets our full attention.

  • Dedicated senior designer on every project
  • Transparent, fixed-fee pricing
  • Trade pricing passed on to you
