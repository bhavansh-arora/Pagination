FAQ

Questions we hear often

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp and we usually reply within a few hours.

Ask on WhatsApp
Do you make equipment to custom sizes?

Yes. Almost everything we build is made to order. Length, height, shelves, burners or compartments, lighting and branding can all be tailored to your space and menu.

What material do you use?

We work in food-grade stainless steel (SS 304 / SS 202 as per budget), toughened glass and branded compressors for refrigeration. Your quotation spells out the options clearly.

Do you deliver outside Nagpur?

Yes. We regularly supply across Vidarbha and Maharashtra and can arrange transport to neighbouring states. Ask us for delivery charges to your city.

How long does manufacturing take?

Standard counters and carts are typically ready in 7–15 days depending on size and workload. Full kitchens are scheduled once the design is final.

Do you offer maintenance and repair?

Yes. Maintenance and repair of refrigeration and kitchen equipment is a core service, for our products and other brands.

Planning a new outlet or kitchen?

Share your space and menu. We'll design, fabricate and install it, made to measure in our Nagpur workshop.
