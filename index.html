Hi there! I'm Mark — your Hawaii wedding videographer. Infused with Filipino warmth and a creative heart, every film I make is centered on you, your love, and the community surrounding you on your biggest day.

Whether your celebration takes you to a Hawaiian beach and mountains, a classic church, or beneath the stars, I'm there to capture the magic — transforming once-in-a-lifetime moments into a story you'll relive for a lifetime.
