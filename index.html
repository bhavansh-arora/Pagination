FAQ

Questions we hear often

Do you make equipment to custom sizes?

Yes. Almost everything we build is made to order — length, height, number of shelves, burners or compartments, lighting and branding can all be tailored to your space and menu.

What material do you use?

We work primarily in food-grade stainless steel (SS 304 / SS 202 as per budget), toughened glass and branded compressors for refrigeration units. We'll explain options clearly in your quotation.

Do you deliver outside Nagpur?

Yes. We regularly supply across Vidarbha and Maharashtra and can arrange transport to neighbouring states. Ask us for delivery charges to your city.

How long does manufacturing take?

Standard counters and carts are typically ready in 7–15 days depending on size and workload. Full kitchens are scheduled after the design is finalised.

Do you offer maintenance and repair?

Yes — maintenance and repair of refrigeration and kitchen equipment is one of our core services, for our own products and other brands.

Planning a new outlet, kitchen or counter?

Share your space and menu — we'll design, fabricate and install it, made to measure in Nagpur.
