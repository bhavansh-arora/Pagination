(07) Good to know

Questions, answered.

Ask a question
01What happens in the free 30-minute session?

It’s a relaxed, confidential conversation. You share what’s going on and what you hope for; I ask a few questions and explain how coaching could help. If it feels right, we choose a path together. If not, you leave with more clarity than you came with.

02Is coaching the same as therapy?

No. Coaching is forward-focused: it helps you gain clarity, build resilience and take action. It doesn’t diagnose or treat mental-health conditions. Many clients work with a coach alongside their therapist or medical team, and I’m always happy to encourage that.

03Who do you work with?

Women navigating fertility, IVF, loss or adoption; people in career or life transition; founders and CEOs; and leaders who want to grow their conscious leadership. One-to-one, and in group journeys.

04How much does coaching cost?

It depends on the path and rhythm we choose together. I’ll walk you through the options transparently in our free first session, with no pressure to decide on the spot.
