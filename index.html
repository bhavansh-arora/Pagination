About Atelier Interiors – a design-and-build studio

We are a small team of designers, carpenters and project managers who believe a good interior should feel calm, work hard and age well. Every project starts with listening: how you cook, where the light falls, what you want to keep.

We handle drawings, materials, manufacturing and installation in-house, so you get one timeline, one point of contact and a finish that matches the render.
