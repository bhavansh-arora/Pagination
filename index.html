Our Philosophy

How we look after your skin

01

The Room

Every visit starts in a calm, softly lit treatment room. We talk first — about your routine, your sleep, what your skin has been doing lately — before we touch anything.

02

The Method

Our therapists are trained in lymphatic and sculpting massage. We work with the muscles and contours of the face, so results come from technique rather than a long list of products.

03

The Formulas

Green clays, rosehip, sea fennel and vitamin C — we mix small batches in our own back room and change the recipes with the seasons, so nothing sits on a shelf for long.

Your Visit

Ninety minutes, four steps

I

Arrive

Herbal tea, a quiet seat and a short chat about how your skin has been feeling.

II

Assess

We look closely under daylight lamps and plan a treatment that suits you today.

III

Treat

One therapist, one hour of hands-on work. Phones away, lights low, nothing hurried.

IV

Take Home

A simple written routine and, if you need them, a few samples to try at home.
