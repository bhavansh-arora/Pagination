Frequently Asked Questions

Still have questions? Message us directly on WhatsApp and we'll respond within minutes.

Ask on WhatsApp

Do I need to book in advance?

Walk-ins are welcome, but we recommend booking 1–2 days ahead — especially on weekends and for groom's packages — to get your preferred barber and time.

What products do you use?

Premium, dermatologically-tested grooming brands for hair, beard and skin — never diluted or low-grade products.

Can I customize a groom's package?

Absolutely — every groom's package is tailored after a complimentary consultation and trial session before the big day.

Do you offer home or venue service?

Yes — groomsmen parties and wedding-day grooming are available on-location for an additional fee. Ask us on WhatsApp for details.
