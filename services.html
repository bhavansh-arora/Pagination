Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a referral to see a specialist?
No referral needed. You can book directly with any of our specialists through our online appointment system or by calling the clinic.
What should I bring to my first appointment?
A valid ID, your insurance card (if applicable), and a list of current medications. New patients should arrive 15 minutes early to complete intake forms.
How quickly can I get lab results?
Most standard blood work and imaging results are available within 24 hours through our on-site diagnostic lab.
Can I reschedule or cancel my appointment?
Yes — you can reschedule or cancel up to 4 hours before your appointment at no charge via the confirmation link or by calling us.
Do you offer telemedicine visits?
Absolutely. Video consultations are available for follow-ups, prescription renewals, and many minor concerns — book the same way as an in-person visit.

Not sure which service you need?

Call our care coordinators — they'll point you to the right specialist in minutes.
