Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How does online booking work?+

Pick a service on our booking page, choose an available date and time, and fill in a few details about your project. You'll get a reference number and a calendar invite instantly — no back-and-forth emails needed.

Do you work outside Mumbai?+

Yes — consultations and full design packages are available remotely by video for clients anywhere. In-home visits are offered within 50 km of Mumbai.

What happens after I book?+

You'll receive a confirmation with everything you told us, plus a short pre-session questionnaire so our designers arrive prepared. Need to reschedule? Just reply to your confirmation email.

Can I cancel or reschedule?+

Absolutely, free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment. See our booking page for full policy details.

Not sure which service is right for you?

Start with a free 20-minute discovery call — we'll point you to the right package for your space and budget.
