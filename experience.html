Sc. 03 Questions

Good questions.

Where are you based — and do you travel?

I'm based on Oahu and film across the islands — Kualoa Ranch, Ko Olina, the North Shore and Honolulu — plus Kona on Hawaii Island and over to Kauai. I also travel for weddings on the mainland: recent films include Salt Lake City, Utah, Mansfield, Texas and a Yosemite elopement.

How far ahead should we book?

As soon as you have a date and venue in mind. I film one wedding per day, so popular dates go first — it's always worth checking availability early.

What will our film feel like?

Like you. Every film is edited around your story, your voices and your people. Watch a few films to get a feel for the style, then tell me what you want to feel when you press play.

Can you film our cultural traditions?

Absolutely. Whether it's a Hawaiian blessing, a lei exchange, the veil, cord and arras, an Iglesia Ni Cristo ceremony or a money dance that takes over the reception, we'll plan around the moments that matter most to your families.

How many weddings do you film in a day?

Just one. On your date, my full attention — and every camera — is on you and your people.

Do you film with drones and multiple cameras?

Yes. Multi-camera coverage keeps vows and speeches covered from every angle, and drone footage shows off your venue from above wherever the location allows it.

What's the investment?

Every collection is tailored to the day you're planning. Share your date and a few details, and I'll send current collections and availability.

What happens after we enquire?

I read every message personally and will reply to set up a relaxed call — a chance to talk story and make sure we're the right fit for each other.
