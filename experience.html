Sc. 03 Questions

Good questions.

Where are you based — and do you travel?

I'm based in Hawaii and film across the islands, from Kaneohe and Ko Olina on Oahu to Kona on Hawaii Island. I also travel for weddings on the mainland and beyond — recent films include a wedding in Salt Lake City, Utah.

How far ahead should we book?

As soon as you have a date and venue in mind. Popular dates go first, so it's always worth checking availability early — even if the rest of your planning is still coming together.

What will our film feel like?

Like you. Every film is edited around your story, your voices and your people. Watch a few films to get a feel for the style, then tell me what you want to feel when you press play.

Can you film our cultural traditions?

Absolutely. Whether it's a Hawaiian blessing, a lei exchange, the veil, cord and arras, or a money dance that takes over the reception, we'll plan around the moments that matter most to your families.

What's the investment?

Every collection is tailored to the day you're planning. Share your date and a few details, and I'll send current collections and availability.

What happens after we enquire?

I read every message personally and will reply to set up a relaxed call — a chance to talk story and make sure we're the right fit for each other.
