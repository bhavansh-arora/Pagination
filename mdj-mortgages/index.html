How much can I borrow?
Most lenders start at around 4 to 4.5 times your income, and some go higher for certain professions or incomes. They also look at your outgoings, credit history, deposit and term. Try the calculator above, then we'll check real lender criteria for you.
What is a decision in principle?
It's a lender's indication of how much it would be willing to lend, based on a soft look at your details. Estate agents often ask for one before you make an offer. It isn't a guarantee of a mortgage.
I'm self-employed. Can I still get a mortgage?
Yes. Lenders use your tax calculations (SA302s) and accounts, and some will consider one year's figures, retained profits or contractor day rates. Picking the right lender for how you're paid makes the biggest difference.
How do I find the best rate?
Rates change often. The lowest rate isn't always the cheapest deal once fees, deal type and term are included. We compare the total cost over the deal period, not just the headline rate.
