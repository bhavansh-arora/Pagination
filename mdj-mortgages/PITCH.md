# MDJ Mortgages — website concept

A one-page concept site in `index.html`. It's a single static file, so it opens in any browser and can be hosted anywhere.

## The three ideas (for the reply to Mark)

1. **Show the borrowing figure before asking for details.** A "How much could you borrow?" calculator sits next to the headline. Visitors get a figure (with a single or joint income) without entering an email. The button under the result takes them into the enquiry form. Visitors who get something useful first are more likely to enquire.
2. **A remortgage countdown.** Visitors pick the month their fixed rate ends and see how many months are left, whether their six-month window is open, and a "Remind me" button. This captures remortgage leads months before the fix ends.
3. **Lead with the reviews and awards, and make contact easy.** The 2026 Best Mortgage Broker award, 5.0 from 186 Google reviews and the FCA status are all visible in the first screen. On phones, a fixed bar keeps **Call / WhatsApp / Free review** one tap away. The enquiry form is three short steps (goal, timing, contact) and can feed straight into Pipedrive.

## What's on the page

- Hero with award chip, headline, Calendly and WhatsApp buttons, and the review score
- Borrowing calculator (4–4.5× income, LTV and buying power)
- Trust strip: Google rating, 2026 award, FCA FRN 949077, Chineham office
- Six situations: first-time buyers, self-employed, moving home, remortgage, later life and equity release, protection
- Fixed-rate countdown with a timeline
- Four-step process
- Award feature band (2026 Quality Business Awards, plus 2024 and 2025)
- Mark Jones profile with a real client quote
- Moving reviews panel using real Google review excerpts
- Fee table (£0 first consultation, £295 typical fee, £1,000 cap)
- Areas covered, FAQs, a three-step enquiry form, £50 referral band
- Full compliance footer: repossession warning, fee disclosure, ERC and equity release risks, Stonebridge AR statement, fraud alert
