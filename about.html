Our story

From a small workshop to a name trusted across Vidarbha

IB Refrigeration & Engineering started in 2007 in Nagpur as a sole-proprietorship workshop with one goal: build commercial kitchen and refrigeration equipment that works hard and looks great.

Today our team of skilled fabricators, welders and refrigeration technicians manufactures a comprehensive range — display counters, commercial refrigerators, gas bhattis, stainless steel tandoors, sinks, racks, wall shelves, food carts, shawarma machines, water coolers and complete restaurant kitchens.

Every product is made from quality raw material sourced from reliable vendors, and finished in our own workshop at Gaddigodam, Kamptee Road. That's why bakeries, hotels, caterers and street-food entrepreneurs keep coming back — and sending their friends.
