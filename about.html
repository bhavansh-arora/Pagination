Sc. 01 The story

Entirely centered on you, your love, and the community surrounding you.

I'm Mark Arista, your Hawaii wedding videographer. Infused with Filipino warmth and creative heart, my films are entirely centered on you, your love, and the community surrounding you on your biggest day.

A wedding is never only about two people. It's the families who flew in, the elders who gave their blessing, and the friends who would do anything for you. That's who I film for — so years from now you can hear the laughter, see the tears and feel the whole day again.

More than an artist, I consider myself a keen observer: a visual storyteller who loves listening to unique love stories. The result is simple — authentic, heartfelt and handmade.

One wedding per dayMulti-camera coverageDrone footageCinematic · documentary · story-led

Sc. 02 What I believe

Three things I never compromise.

i.

You're the story

No two films look alike, because no two couples are alike. The edit is built around your voices, your vows and your people.

ii.

Presence over poses

I'd rather you live your day than perform it. I stay close, calm and unobtrusive, so the real moments happen on their own.

iii.

Community matters

The hug from your lola, the toast from your best friend, the whole family on the dance floor — they all belong in the film.

Sc. 03 Where I film

Based in Hawaii. Ready to travel.

Oahu is home — from Kualoa Ranch to Ko Olina and the North Shore. Neighbor islands like Hawaii Island and Kauai, and mainland trips to Utah, Texas and Yosemite, are all part of the story.
