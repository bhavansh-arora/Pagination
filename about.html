Our Story

From a single exam room to a full-service clinic

In 2008, Dr. Ananya Sharma opened Meridian with one exam room and a conviction: healthcare should never feel rushed. Word spread quickly — not because of marketing, but because patients felt genuinely cared for.

Today, Meridian is a full-service clinic with 24 specialists, an on-site diagnostic lab, and over 12,400 patients who call us their medical home. The exam room philosophy hasn't changed — only our capacity to deliver it at scale.

18 years of growth, one patient at a time

A quick look at how Meridian evolved from a single room into a trusted community institution.

2008

Meridian opens its doors

Dr. Sharma opens a single-room family practice with a focus on unhurried, personal care.

2013

On-site diagnostic lab launches

Same-day blood work and imaging results reduce patient wait times dramatically.

2018

Specialist departments expand

Cardiology, neurology, and orthopedics join the practice, rounding out full-family care.

2023

Telemedicine goes live

Virtual consultations bring Meridian's care to patients who can't make it in person.

2026

12,400+ patients strong
