Who We Are

More than a haircut — a ritual.

CROWN & BLADE opened in 2013 as a two-chair shop with one rule: do the job properly or don't do it at all. Today we're an 18-barber team serving over 9,600 regulars — but that founding rule hasn't changed.

Every cut starts with a real consultation and ends with a hot towel. No rushed jobs, no shortcuts.
