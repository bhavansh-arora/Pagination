Chapter i.

Rochester, New York

I grew up in Rochester, a city with a deep connection to photography, thanks in part to Kodak. My dad worked there. I didn't realise it at the time, but being surrounded by a culture of imagery and innovation planted a seed that would later shape my career.

Chapter ii.

A trip to Ireland

The moment came when I bought a nice camera for a trip to Ireland. I just wanted better photos of the landscapes and history I'd always dreamed of seeing. Instead, I fell in love with photography: composition, light, and the way a single frame can hold so much emotion.

That trip changed everything, and soon a hobby became something much more meaningful.

Chapter iii.

Today, together

Over the years, Lydia and I have built a full photography and video production team, capturing everything from weddings to commercial projects. Weddings have become our passion: the energy, the emotion and the once-in-a-lifetime moments.

We've grown into a team couples trust to capture their day exactly as it happens.
