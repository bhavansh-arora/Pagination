Who We Are

More than a salon — a ritual.

VELVET began in 2013 as a two-chair studio with one belief: beauty services should feel like an art form, not a transaction. Today, we're an award-winning team of 24 specialists serving over 8,400 clients — but that founding belief hasn't changed.

Every colour, cut, facial and bridal look is treated as a collaboration between you and our artists — never a template.
