How we started

From a one-woman studio to a team of thirteen

Atelier Interiors began in a small studio in Dadar, with founder Marguerite Laurent taking on friends-of-friends projects between architecture contracts. Word spread quickly — not because the rooms looked expensive, but because they looked right.

Today the studio is a close-knit team of designers, a procurement lead and a project manager, still taking on a deliberately limited number of projects each season so nothing — and no one — falls through the cracks.
