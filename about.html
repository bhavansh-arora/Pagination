Our story

We exist for the moment the room goes silent.

That breathless second before the applause. The gasp when the doors open. Since 2011 we've chased that feeling in every event we create.

Since 2011

Born from a passion for wonder.

Aurelia began as two friends with a borrowed van and a belief that events should feel like art, not admin.

Fifteen years later we're a team of 60 designers, producers, technologists and hosts — but the philosophy hasn't changed. We obsess over the details nobody notices, so everyone notices the magic. We say no to more events than we accept, because every client deserves our very best.
