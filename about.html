(01)The story

Hello,I'm Sam.

Chef, pâtissier and artisan baker. With specialist training at Le Cordon Bleu New Zealand and The School of Artisan Food, and years of international experience, I created Love to Cook to share both craft and joy in the kitchen.

Cook with me Read the story

Food has always been part of my story. Growing up in a Scottish–Portuguese family, meals were more than nourishment they were where life happened.

(02)The journey

Seven chapters, three countries, one kitchen.

From a student catering business to Le Cordon Bleu's teaching kitchen, and home to Crieff.

Chapter 01Scotland & Portugal

A table where life happened

Growing up in a Scottish–Portuguese home, where family and flavour came alive around every meal.

Chapter 02United Kingdom

The spark: a student catering business

Self-taught cooking turned into a catering business while still a student, and a path was set.

Chapter 03UK & Europe

Historic houses, private dining, big events

Years cooking in historic houses, for private dining and at large-scale events, before stepping into teaching and food education.

Chapter 04New Zealand

Love to Cook is born

A home-based cookery school designed to make cooking approachable and inspiring.

Chapter 05Le Cordon Bleu NZ

Pâtisserie, with distinction

Specialised at Le Cordon Bleu, graduating with distinction, then joined their culinary team as a Pâtisserie Chef Lecturer.

Chapter 06The School of Artisan Food

Chapter 07Crieff, Scotland

Home: 16 King Street

Cook school, bakery and pâtisserie, bringing everything together to share with you.

(03)Today

Everything I've learned, shared with you.

Today, through Love to Cook in Crieff, I bring together everything I've learned: creating delicious bread and pastries, sharing knowledge teaching cuisine and pâtisserie, and helping others discover confidence and joy in the kitchen.

Classes are deliberately small (never more than eight) because the best way to learn a craft is with someone beside you, adjusting your hands and explaining why, not just how.

(04)What I believe

Craft, confidence & joy.

i.

Craft

Proper technique, explained clearly. The same standards I taught at Le Cordon Bleu, made friendly for a home kitchen.

ii.

Confidence

Small groups, real hands-on time and honest feedback, so you leave knowing you can do it again at home.

iii.

Joy

Food is where life happens. Every class should feel like a gathering: generous, warm and fun.
