Our Story

From one small clinic to Austin's most-loved dental studio

Dentava opened its doors in 2010 with a single treatment room and a simple mission — deliver hospital-grade dentistry inside a space that feels more spa than sterile clinic. Sixteen years later, we've grown to eight specialists and three treatment suites, but the mission hasn't changed.

Every material, process and interaction is chosen with one question in mind: would we want this for our own family? If the answer is anything less than yes, we don't ship it.
