(03)Getting here & policies

The small print, kept small.

Getting to Crieff

Love to Cook is at 16 King Street in the centre of Crieff, Perthshire: around 30 minutes from Perth and roughly an hour from Glasgow and Edinburgh. Town-centre parking is a short walk away.

Booking & cancellations

Transfer your seat to another date free of charge up to 7 days before your class. Inside 7 days we'll try to resell your place from the waitlist. If we ever need to cancel, you'll get a full refund or a new date of your choice.

Bakery collection

Order by Wednesday 6pm for Friday or Saturday collection between 9am and 1pm. Uncollected orders can't be refunded, but give us a call and we'll always try to help.

Privacy

We only use your details to manage your booking and, if you opt in, to send new class dates. Payments are processed securely by Square; we never see or store your card details.
