Frequently asked questions

What should I bring to my first visit?
A photo ID, your insurance card (if applicable), and a list of any current medications. We'll handle the rest.

Yes — free covered parking is available directly behind the building, with dedicated visitor spots.
Can I reschedule or cancel online?
Absolutely — every confirmation email includes a link to reschedule or cancel with at least 24 hours notice.
