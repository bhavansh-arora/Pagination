Before You Visit

A few things patients often ask

Is parking available on-site?
Yes, free patient parking is available directly behind the building, with dedicated accessible spots near the entrance.
Do you accept walk-in patients?
We prioritize booked appointments, but walk-ins are accommodated for urgent concerns whenever a same-day slot is open.
How do I verify my insurance is accepted?
Call our front desk with your insurance provider and plan number — our team will confirm coverage before your visit.
What do I do in a medical emergency?
For life-threatening emergencies, call your local emergency number immediately or go to the nearest emergency room. Meridian Care Clinic is an outpatient facility.
