FAQ

Common questions.

What's included when I buy a site like Beauty?

Every sale includes the full source code, design assets, and the domain (or subdomain, where noted). We'll also agree on a handoff window for questions and a walkthrough of the codebase.

Can I customize a site after I buy it?

Absolutely — every sale includes the full source code, so you're free to re-brand, extend or restyle it however you like. We're also happy to help with that work directly if you'd rather not do it yourself.

Can you build a new site for me instead?

Absolutely — client builds are Code Bunny's core business. Tell us about your project in the form above and we'll follow up with a scope and timeline.

How fast do you typically deliver?

It depends on scope, but most landing pages and templates ship in 1–2 weeks, with larger builds scoped individually.
