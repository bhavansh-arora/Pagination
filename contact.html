All FAQs
How much is the deposit?

A non-refundable $1,100 NZD deposit locks in your date, and nobody else can book it after that. The balance is settled closer to the wedding.

How long until our film is delivered?

Usually 8–12 weeks, for every package. If you can't wait, add the Next-day Teaser and you'll have a short film within 24 hours.

How many hours do you film on the day?

There are no set coverage hours. I'm there for whatever your story needs, and I plan the timing around your photographer's schedule.

Do you travel?

Yes, anywhere. Travel outside greater Christchurch has costs, which I'll quote upfront when you enquire.
